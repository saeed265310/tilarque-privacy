1. Introduction

Tilarque ("the app", "we", "our") is a sliding image puzzle game available on Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use Tilarque, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By using Tilarque, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide (Optional)

Tilarque does not require you to create an account. You can play fully anonymously. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive:

  • Display name – your name as provided by Google or Apple
  • Email address – your email as provided by Google or Apple
  • Profile photo URL – if provided by your sign-in provider

This information is only used to identify your account within the app. Sign-in is entirely optional.

2.3 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Game progress, settings, and puzzle history are stored locally on your device using Hive (an offline database). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable cloud save via Google Play Games or Game Center.

5. Advertising

Tilarque displays ads powered by Google AdMob. Ads are shown to users who have not purchased an ad-free subscription or lifetime access.

AdMob may use your Advertising ID and approximate location to show you personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (Android 12+) or opt out of ads personalisation
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

If you purchase an ad-free subscription or lifetime access, ads are removed entirely and your Advertising ID is no longer used by the app.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Tilarque offers optional in-app purchases managed through your device's app store and RevenueCat:

  • Monthly subscription – removes ads, unlocks subscriber features
  • Annual subscription – removes ads, unlocks subscriber and premium features
  • Lifetime purchase – removes ads, unlocks premium features, one-time payment
  • Coin packs – in-game virtual currency (Tilarque Coins) for use within the app

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and subscription status to manage your entitlements across devices.

Tilarque Coins are virtual currency with no monetary value outside the app. They cannot be transferred, sold, or converted to cash.
